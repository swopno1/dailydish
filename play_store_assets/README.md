# Daily Dish — Google Play Store Assets

This directory contains the production-ready visual marketing and publishing assets for **Daily Dish**, published by **ViveScript Solutions LLC**.

## Asset Inventory

### 1. App Icon (`/play_store_assets/app-icon/`)
* **`app-icon.png`**: High-resolution 512×512 PNG app icon (32-bit color, no transparency, compliant with Google Play specification).
* **`app-icon-foreground.png`**: 512×512 foreground graphic layer featuring the culinary cloche, gourmet dish, and chef hat insignia.
* **`app-icon-background.png`**: 512×512 solid `#D35400` terracotta culinary background layer.

### 2. Feature Graphic (`/play_store_assets/feature-graphic/`)
* **`feature-graphic.png`**: 1024×500 PNG promotional banner. Features appetizing rustic culinary photography with ambient warm lighting, fresh herbs, and dishes.

### 3. Phone Screenshots (`/play_store_assets/phone/`)
* **`phone-01.png`**: 1080×1920 portrait screenshot showcasing the Recipe of the Day hero display with preparation time, cook time, and action buttons.
* **`phone-02.png`**: 1080×1920 portrait screenshot highlighting the interactive Ingredients Checklist and Servings Scaler (2, 4, 6, 8 persons).
* **`phone-03.png`**: 1080×1920 portrait screenshot showing Step-by-Step Cooking Instructions and the Chef's Secret Tip callout card.
* **`phone-04.png`**: 1080×1920 portrait screenshot displaying the 1-Tap Recipe Share modal with WhatsApp and social media actions, along with bilingual English/Bangla language switching.

### 4. Tablet Screenshots (`/play_store_assets/tablet/`)
* **`tablet-01.png`**: 1920×1200 landscape tablet layout demonstrating adaptive side-by-side recipe presentation.
* **`tablet-02.png`**: 1920×1200 landscape tablet layout highlighting the recipe discovery archive and favorites collection.
