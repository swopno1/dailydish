### Full Description (ASO & Conversion Optimized)

Looking for fresh meal inspiration without getting lost in complicated recipe websites? 

Welcome to **Daily Dish**, your delightfully simple daily cooking companion. Every day, discover one featured, carefully curated recipe complete with mouth-watering photography, exact ingredients, prep and cooking times, and clear step-by-step instructions.

Whether you're cooking for yourself, hosting dinner for the family, or sharing kitchen inspiration with friends, Daily Dish makes cooking stress-free, engaging, and fun.

---

### ✨ KEY FEATURES:

🍳 **Recipe of the Day**  
Wake up to a brand-new featured culinary dish every single day. From aromatic Biryani and creamy Tuscan pasta to sizzling garlic salmon and authentic Bengali mustard curries, discover varied cuisines designed for home cooks.

🛒 **Interactive Ingredients Checklist**  
Never forget a pantry staple. Dynamic ingredient checklists let you check off items as you prep your kitchen counter.

👥 **Dynamic Servings Scaler**  
Cooking for 2, 4, 6, or 8 people? Switch servings with one tap and ingredient quantities scale automatically in real-time.

👨‍🍳 **Step-by-Step Cooking Steps**  
Follow numbered, beginner-friendly instructions with helpful completion progress markers so you never lose your place while at the stove.

💡 **Chef's Pro Tips**  
Unlock culinary secrets from experienced chefs—from crisping salmon skin to maximizing spice aromas.

📤 **1-Tap Share to WhatsApp & Social Apps**  
Share beautifully formatted recipe cards directly to family and friends over WhatsApp, Messenger, Facebook, or your favorite messaging apps with a single tap.

📋 **Quick Copy Recipe**  
Copy complete, formatted recipes to your clipboard for quick grocery lists or notes.

🌐 **Bilingual Experience (English & বাংলা)**  
Seamlessly switch between English and Bengali with one tap. Every single recipe, ingredient, and cooking method is fully translated and localized.

❤️ **Favorites & Recipe History**  
Save the dishes you loved to your Favorites collection or browse past featured recipes anytime.

📶 **100% Offline-First**  
Cook anywhere, anytime. Daily Dish works completely offline without requiring an account, login, or internet connection.
