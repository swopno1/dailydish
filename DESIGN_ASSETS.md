# Design Assets & Visual Identity — Daily Dish

**Product:** Daily Dish  
**Company:** ViveScript Solutions LLC  
**Design System:** Material Design 3 (M3)  

---

## 1. Brand Visual Direction

* **Vibe:** Warm, artisanal culinary aesthetic that evokes freshly prepared gourmet meals, home cooking comfort, and appetite appeal.
* **Dominant Color:** Terracotta Orange (`#D35400`) & Deep Saffron (`#E65100`)
* **Secondary Color:** Warm Saffron Gold (`#C57D22`)
* **Accent/Tertiary:** Fresh Sage / Rosemary Green (`#2E7D32`)
* **Backgrounds:** Warm Cream (`#FAF7F2`) in Light Mode, Dark Espresso (`#171311`) in Dark Mode

## 3. Typography & UI Density
* **Display Font:** Material 3 Display Medium & Title Large (bold headers with high legibility)
* **Body Font:** High-contrast body text for comfortable kitchen reading while standing at the counter
* **Grid Spacing:** Strict 8.dp Material spacing system with 16.dp screen gutters and 48.dp minimum touch targets.
