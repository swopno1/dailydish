## 1. Introduction

ViveScript Solutions LLC ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our Android mobile application **Daily Dish** handles information when you install and use the app.

Daily Dish is designed with an **offline-first, privacy-conscious philosophy**. The application provides daily featured recipes, cooking instructions, and recipe sharing without requiring an account, sign-in, cloud backend, or personal profile.

---

## 2. Information We DO NOT Collect

Daily Dish does **not** collect, store, or transmit:
* **Personal Identifiable Information (PII):** We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, home addresses, or passwords.
* **Account Credentials:** No account creation or authentication is required or supported.
* **Location Data:** We do not request or access GPS, coarse location, or background location.
* **Contacts or Media:** We do not access your address book, photos, microphone, or camera.
* **Financial Data:** Daily Dish is free to use and does not collect payment card information.

---

## 3. Information Handled Locally on Your Device

To provide a smooth culinary experience, Daily Dish saves small non-identifying preferences locally on your device via standard Android `SharedPreferences`:
* **Language Preference:** Your choice of English or Bangla (বাংলা).
* **Saved Favorites:** Local identifiers of recipes you have bookmarked.
* **Cooking Checklist State:** Temporary checkboxes for ingredients and completed cooking steps.

**All local preference data remains strictly on your device** and is never uploaded to any server. Uninstalling the application or clearing app data deletes this information completely.

---

## 4. Recipe Sharing

When you use the "Share Recipe" or "Copy Recipe" feature:
* Recipe text is copied to your device's clipboard or passed to Android's native system share sheet.
* If you choose to share via third-party applications (such as WhatsApp, Messenger, Facebook, or SMS), your interaction with those apps is governed by their respective privacy policies. Daily Dish does not monitor or store shared messages.

---

## 5. Third-Party Services & Advertising (Google AdMob)

Daily Dish integrates the official **Google Mobile Ads SDK** (AdMob) to display non-intrusive advertisements. 

When your device has active internet connectivity, Google AdMob may automatically collect non-personal device identifiers and diagnostic telemetry in compliance with Google Play developer policies:
* **Device Identifiers:** Advertising ID (GAID), device model, operating system version.
* **Performance Data:** Diagnostic metrics to measure ad impressions and prevent fraudulent clicks.

For further information regarding how Google processes advertising data, please refer to Google's Privacy Policy:  
[https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

---

## 6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Daily Dish is intended for general audiences interested in cooking. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

---

## 7. Data Retention & Deletion

Since we do not collect personal data on external servers, there is no remote data to retain or delete. You can delete all locally stored preferences at any time by clearing the application storage in your Android device settings or uninstalling the app.
